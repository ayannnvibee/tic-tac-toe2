Privacy Policy

Tic Tac Toe Infinity · Last updated: 22 October 2026

Overview

Tic Tac Toe Infinity ("the game") is published by Kodrilla ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information is used when you play and why. We built the game to collect as little as possible.

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics and no server of its own. Your games, scores and levels are never saved or sent anywhere; every launch starts fresh.

Two small settings are stored only on your device: whether you have accepted this policy and the Terms of Use, and whether you have seen the intro. They are deleted when you uninstall the game.

Advertising

The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, a service of Google. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use information from your device, such as its advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device type and how you interact with ads. Google may use this to show you ads that are more relevant to you.

This information goes directly to Google; we do not receive or store it. Google describes how it uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and its privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads at any time in your phone's settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your phone).

Internet access

Gameplay works fully offline. The game only uses the internet to load ads. Without a connection the game works normally, just without ads.

Children

The game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security and retention

Because we do not collect personal information, we do not keep any. The two on-device settings stay until you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If the change matters, the game will ask you to review and accept it again. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Visit kodrilla.com/legal for ways to contact Kodrilla.
