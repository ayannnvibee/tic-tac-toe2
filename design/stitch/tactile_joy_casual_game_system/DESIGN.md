## Brand & Style

The brand personality is exuberant, tactile, and instantly welcoming. Crafted specifically for casual mobile gameplay, the interface prioritizes immediate physical affordance, crisp feedback, and an uplifting emotional response. It evokes the polished, cheerful atmosphere of premium mobile titles—inviting repeat sessions without cognitive fatigue.

### Visual Style
The design style is a refined hybrid of **Tactile/Skeuomorphic Modernism** and **Playful Neo-Casual Flat**. It rejects dark themes, heavy glassmorphism, and noisy skeuomorphic textures in favor of clean vectors, deliberate chunky bevels, crisp bottom-rim 3D button lip details, and soft warm ambient shadows. Surfaces feel springy, interactable, and physical. Generous whitespace balances vibrant brand pigments, preserving focus on the central game board while celebrating micro-moments with lush pastel accents.

## Colors

The palette establishes a vivid hierarchy built upon a gentle warm-cream foundation, preventing harsh glare while allowing energetic pigments to pop.

### Palette Architecture
- **Canvas Base (`#FFFDF7`):** Soft warm white providing a friendly, paper-bright environment that reduces eye strain compared to clinical `#FFFFFF`.
- **Card & Cell Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** Crisp pure white layers lifted above the warm-cream base to establish distinct interactive planes.
- **Primary Purple (`#8B5CF6`):** The master brand anchor, governing navigational elements, crown banners, and primary CTA accents.
- **Player X Electric Blue (`#3B82F6`):** Dynamic, competitive player indicator with high luminosity and clarity.
- **Computer / Opponent O Citrus Orange (`#FF8A3D`):** Warm, energetic contrast against blue, delivering balanced readability across the game grid.
- **Victory & Positive Affirmation (`#22C55E`):** Pure fresh green for win states, reward tallies, and unlock prompts.
- **Pastel Palette (Micro-surfaces & badges):** Soft Lavender (`#EDE9FE`), Pastel Sky (`#DBEAFE`), Soft Peach (`#FED7AA`), and Buttercup Yellow (`#FEF08A`) used for badge fills, category tags, streak counters, and decorative star bursts.
- **Text & Structure Hierarchy:** Deep ink-plum `#2E1065` and dark slate `#1E293B` for bold headlines; balanced muted slate `#64748B` for secondary stats; pale lavender `#E2E8F0` / `#DDD6FE` for borders.

## Typography

The typography leverages `Nunito Sans` universally across headlines, controls, indicators, and numeric readouts. Its rounded terminal geometry reinforces the friendly, bouncy aesthetic while high weights (800 and 900) grant gameplay counters the weight required to command instant glanceability during high-speed play.

### Typographic Principles
- **Weight as Hierarchy:** Reserve 800/900 weights for scoreboards, win labels, modal heads, and button actions. Body text remains at 600 weight to prevent stroke thinning on mobile high-DPI displays.
- **Tight Leading on Headlines:** Headlines employ slightly compressed leading to create unified sticker-like textual lockups.
- **Tabular Numerals for Game HUDs:** Numeric scores, turn counters, and round timers must rely on proportional figure alignments with fixed height baselines to prevent UI jitter during score ticks.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built for native mobile single-screen ergonomics, centering the interactive arena within the thumb-accessible sweet spot.

### Grid & Form Factors
- **Mobile Handset (Base Portrait):** A single-column vertical layout bordered by `1rem` safe-area canvas margins. The grid matrix expands to a maximal responsive square bound between 320px and 390px, with `gutter-sm` (12px) internal cell separation.
- **Tablet / Large Mobile:** The layout transitions to a centered, fixed-constraint frame (max width 480px for the play field) with `1.25rem` canvas margins, maintaining ergonomic touch distances without over-stretching the play pieces.
- **Component Stack Rhythm:** Spacing adopts multiples of 4px/8px. Tight element relations (icon to badge copy) use `space-xs` (4px) or `space-sm` (8px); standard card insets use `space-md` (16px); section gaps between header HUD, game board, and bottom control pods use `space-lg` (24px) to `space-xl` (32px).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through tactile vertical offsets, layered planar cards, and dual-tone lip borders rather than blurred realistic drop-shadows.

### Layer Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Playfield Floor):** Canvas base `#FFFDF7`, flat and unshadowed.
- **Tier 1 (Surface Cards & Inactive Grid Cells):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surfaces featuring a 1.5px border of `#EDE9FE` or `#F1F5F9` and an ambient warm drop shadow: `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.08), 0 2px 4px -1px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.03)`.
- **Tier 2 (Interactive 3D Tokens & Buttons):** Elevated using a physical 3.5px solid bottom bevel border (shade +25% deeper than component fill) combined with an immediate elevation shadow: `0 6px 16px -2px rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.16)`.
- **Tier 3 (Floating Overlays & Victory Modals):** High-impact white modal dialogs with a warm-tinted diffused drop: `0 16px 36px -4px rgba(46, 16, 101, 0.14)`. Accompanied by a semi-transparent tinted backdrop: `rgba(46, 16, 101, 0.35)` with zero blur to maintain clean sharpness.
- **Press State Mechanics:** On tap, tactile buttons and cells translate down 3px vertically, collapsing their bottom lip shadow to replicate physical mechanical compression.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a pill-centric, hyper-rounded geometry (`roundedness: 3`). Corners are intentionally generous, eliminating aggressive edges to foster an inviting, toy-like ambiance.

- **Primary Action Buttons & Status Badges:** Fully pill-shaped (`9999px` border-radius).
- **Cards, Modals, & Floating Dialogs:** Rounded 24px to 32px (`rounded-2xl` to `rounded-3xl`), producing soft, cushion-like containment surfaces.
- **Game Grid Cells:** Square 1:1 aspect containers rounded to 20px–24px, ensuring that mark glyphs (X and O) inhabit nested, pebble-like niches.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Tactile Chunky Primary (Brand Purple / Action Green):** Pill or 20px rounded capsule with smooth gradient fills (e.g., `#9333EA` to `#8B5CF6`). Grounded by a solid 4px bottom border lip in deep purple (`#6D28D9` or `#15803D` for green). Text is uppercase or title case, bold Nunito Sans, pure white with subtle text drop shadow `0 1px 2px rgba(0,0,0,0.2)`.
- **Secondary / Utility Button:** Crisp `#FFFFFF` surface, 2px border in `#E2E8F0`, 3px bottom lip in `#CBD5E1`, with dark slate label `#1E293B`.
- **Icon Buttons (Settings, Audio, Restart):** Circular pill shapes (48x48px) with internal bevels, housing centered vector iconography.

### The Tic Tac Toe Interactive Cell
- **Resting State:** White container `#FFFFFF`, 1.5px border `#EDE9FE`, 3px bottom bevel `#DDD6FE`.
- **Filled State - Player X:** Vibrant `#3B82F6` token icon with rounded line caps and a smooth drop bevel; optional pastel blue `#DBEAFE` background wash.
- **Filled State - Player O:** Citrus `#FF8A3D` ring with dynamic stroke thickness and deep orange bevel; optional peach `#FED7AA` background wash.
- **Winning Vector Highlight:** Cell fills transition to Buttercup Yellow `#FEF08A` with a bouncy scaling pulse and glowing outline `#FBBF24`.

### Scoreboard & HUD Pods
- **Versus Banner:** Floating card split into Player X and Player O sectors, separated by a pill badge containing "VS". Active player side scales up +4% with an illuminated active indicator dot.
- **Streak & Coin Badges:** Pill containers featuring soft pastel fills (`#FEF08A` for coins, `#FED7AA` for streaks), containing 16px illustrative badges and bold compact typography.

### Modals & Victory Sheets
- **Celebration Cards:** Pure `#FFFFFF` surfaces with 28px corners, crowned with dynamic ribbon illustrations or confetti bursts.
- **Stat Readouts:** Structured in vertical rows using alternating soft pastel background strips (`#EDE9FE` and `#FFFDF7`) with 12px inner radii.

### Toggle Controls & Chips
- **Mode Selectors (Solo vs AI, Pass & Play, Online):** Segmented pill bar with a floating sliding white pill card that pops forward with a 2px lip and warm shadow.
- **Status Chips:** Small pill containers (24px height) utilizing 11px uppercase label tokens with +0.04em letter-spacing.
